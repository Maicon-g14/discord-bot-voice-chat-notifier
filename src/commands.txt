Currently my available commands are:
 - `ping` Sends a confirmation if the bot is still alive.
 - `toggle-notifier` Activates or deactivates personal notification when someone enters or exits a voicechat.
 - `toggle-global-notifier` Activates or deactivates global notification when someone enters a voicechat.
 - `view-status` Shows the Global Notifier and your Personal Notifier statuses.
